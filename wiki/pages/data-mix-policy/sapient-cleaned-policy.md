# Sapient Cleaned Policy

Part of [Data Mix Policy](/pages/data-mix-policy.md).

Sapient cleaned data is included only after source filtering.

Broad exclusions:

- ReClor
- SciBench
- ScienceQA
- most FLAN
- most Tasksource

Narrow FLAN/Tasksource allow overrides are documented in
[Source Filtering](/pages/source-filtering.md).
