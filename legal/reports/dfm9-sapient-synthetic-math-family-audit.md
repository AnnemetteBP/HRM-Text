# DFM9 Sapient Synthetic and Math Family Audit

Status: engineering/legal evidence triage, not legal advice. Audit date:
2026-08-17.

## Result

The four retained families contribute **6,639,496,414.6 tokens per DFM9
epoch**, or 31.1% of the 21.380B-token Sapient aggregate. All four are cleared
for the current academic/non-commercial scientific-research use without
Article 3 reliance.

The initial 2026-08-17 audit retained a narrow Article 3 fallback for possible
database selection or arrangement rights in Sudoku's unlicensed community
collections. That conclusion is superseded for the current scope by the
project owner's 2026-08-17 manual low-risk acceptance. The missing source
licences and possible compilation-right caveat remain documented; the decision
does not assert that those rights cannot exist and must be revisited for a
different use or release scope.

## PleIAs SYNTH

The local pipeline downloads `PleIAs/SYNTH`, keeps English rows, removes
cooking and Pleias self-knowledge seeds, and maps `query` and
`synthetic_answer` to instruction/response fields. The source card declares
CC-BY-4.0, identifies CC-BY-SA Wikipedia/Structured Wikipedia seeds, and states
that the generators permit output reuse. The filtered conversion does not add
another upstream source.

Result: direct open-licence basis. Preserve PleIAs/Wikipedia attribution and
assess the scope of CC-BY-SA obligations when distributing data derivatives.

## DeepMind Mathematics

The 168 local files are direct conversions of the `train-easy`,
`train-medium`, and `train-hard` files generated by DeepMind's
`mathematics_dataset`. The official package describes a synthetic school-level
mathematics generator and declares Apache-2.0.

Result: direct open-licence basis. The prompts and answers are procedurally
generated mathematical expressions and short functional language.

## AMPS Mathematica

The local converter reads only `amps/mathematica/<topic>/<task>` records from
the AMPS archive linked by the official `hendrycks/math` repository. The MATH
paper says the authors designed roughly 100 Mathematica scripts, 37 with
step-by-step solutions, and generated about 50,000 exercises per script. The
repository is MIT licensed and explicitly links the AMPS pretraining archive.

Result: treat the authors' linked generated corpus as covered by the MIT
release. Preserve the MIT notice and MATH/AMPS citation. The fact that the
archive is linked externally rather than committed is retained as a scope
caveat, not an Article 3 trigger for the current use.

## Sudoku Extreme

The local converter copies all training puzzles from
`sapientinc/sudoku-extreme`, normalizes blank cells, and adds a short fixed
instruction. The card identifies tdoku benchmark data and Enjoy Sudoku/community
collections, then exact-deduplicates and randomly permutes rows, columns,
boxes, and digits. Tdoku's repository, including its data archive and generator,
is BSD-2-Clause. The Sapient card declares no overall dataset licence and does
not establish terms for every named forum/community collection.

An individual Sudoku grid and its mechanically determined solution are
predominantly rules, facts, and functional constraints rather than original
literary expression. A sufficiently substantial or original source
compilation could nevertheless carry database or selection/arrangement
rights. The project owner manually accepts that residual risk for the current
academic/non-commercial scientific-research use. Article 3 / Danish section
11 c is therefore not invoked for this family; Article 4 remains only a
conditional alternative and is not the working basis.

Tasksource are also resolved in
`legal/reports/dfm9-sapient-flan-tasksource-platypus-audit.md`; only the
